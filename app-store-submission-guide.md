## 🎯 Submission Guidelines

### **APKPure Submission:**

1. **Account Setup:**
   - Register at: https://developer.apkpure.com/
   - Use business email for credibility
   - Complete developer profile fully

2. **APK Requirements:**
   - File: Use signed release APK from `android/app/build/outputs/apk/release/`
   - Size: Under 2GB (your app will be much smaller)
   - Format: Standard APK file

3. **Metadata:**
   - **Category:** Health & Fitness
   - **Description:** Use APKPure description above
   - **Screenshots:** 5-8 high-quality screenshots
   - **Privacy Policy:** Link to your privacy policy page

4. **Review Process:**
   - Manual review: Up to 3 business days
   - Focus on safety and user benefit in submission

#### **Option A: Aptoide Connect (Recommended)**

   - Complete business verification

2. **Submission:**
   - **Category:** Lifestyle or Health & Fitness
   - **Age Rating:** Everyone
   - **Description:** Use Aptoide description above
   - **Revenue Share:** 75-90% (industry-leading)

3. **Benefits:**
   - Distribution across 10+ app stores simultaneously
   - Professional tax handling
   - Higher revenue potential

#### **Option B: Traditional Aptoide**

1. **Setup:**
   - Download Aptoide app on Android device
